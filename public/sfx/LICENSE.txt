================================================================================
SFX samples in this folder — Kenney audio packs, CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
================================================================================

The .ogg files in this folder are a hand-picked subset of three sound packs by
Kenney (https://kenney.nl), fetched from their GitHub ports:

  - "Digital Audio"      -> github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-digital-audio-for-godot
  - "Sci-Fi Sounds"      -> github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-for-godot
  - "Impact Sounds"      -> github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot

Kenney releases these packs under CC0 1.0 Universal: they may be used for any
purpose, including commercial, with no attribution required. The full CC0 legal
text ships inside each source repository (file "LICENSE"). Attribution is given
here voluntarily:

  Sound effects: Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0 1.0 Universal.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
